Privacy Policy

Paathshala ERP · CTRL Shift Solutions

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Paathshala ERP ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by CTRL Shift Solutions, operates the school management software and services. This Privacy Policy informs school administrators, teachers, parents, and students of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using our software.

2. Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

Each school tenant's data is strictly partitioned in isolated databases/documents. Student records, fee collections, attendance history, and staff information belonging to one institution are never shared with or accessible by other institutions.

3. Information Collection and Storage

We only store operational school data entered by authorized school staff, including:

  • Student records (names, classes, roll numbers, guardian contacts, emergency addresses)
  • Fee records, transaction receipts, and dues status
  • Staff attendance records, timetable schedules, and academic marks

4. Data Security

All communications are encrypted using modern TLS/SSL standards. Data is safeguarded with role-based access control and enterprise Firestore rules.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data requests, please contact our support team at support@ctrlshifts.in.
