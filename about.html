Empowering Education in Bharat

Built by CTRL Shift Solutions

At CTRL Shift Solutions, we believe that high-quality, enterprise-grade software should not be a privilege reserved only for elite institutions in metropolitan hubs. Our mission is to bridge the digital divide by delivering robust, multi-tenant ERP software specifically designed for schools in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, focusing heavily on regions like Jharkhand and Bihar.

Educational institutions in these growing hubs often face unique challenges, from inconsistent internet connectivity and power outages to the overhead of manually managing student databases and fee ledger logs on paper registers. Our platforms are customized from the ground up to solve these exact hurdles.

High-Speed ERP

Engineered using lightweight ES modules and asynchronous loading for lightning-fast speeds on any device.

Offline PWA

Installable on mobile/desktop with offline-first service worker caching. Keeps records safe even when offline.

Data Isolation

True multi-tenant structure. Separate Cloud Firestore database sandboxing guarantees zero data bleeding.

Our Long-term Vision

We aim to empower school administrators, teachers, and parents with automated systems so they can focus on what truly matters: providing children with a high-quality education.
