School Implementation Story

Success Story: Shishu Vikash Mandir

How a private K-12 school in Bokaro modernized its daily academic and financial operations with Paathshala.

The Challenge: High Friction in Manual Paper Records

Shishu Vikash Mandir (Bokaro, Jharkhand) serves over 280 enrolled students across Nursery to higher grades. For years, the school’s daily attendance, academic timetable, student profiles, and monthly fee collections were managed entirely on paper registers and handwritten receipts.

This manual workflow created constant administrative overhead: tracking errors in fee ledgers, occasional parent fee disputes over missing paper receipts, weeks of manual mark calculations during exam report card season, and severe vulnerability to physical register damage or loss.

The Solution: Paathshala ERP Deployment

The school implemented Paathshala by Ctrl Shift Solutions. Existing student rosters and opening ledger balances were digitized and imported within 24 hours. The platform was deployed across teacher smartphones and administrative computers using an offline-first Progressive Web Application architecture backed by Google Cloud Firebase.

Teachers with varying degrees of tech experience adopted the intuitive, messaging-app-style interface immediately. Staff attendance was linked to GPS geofencing, while fee payments instantly generated bilingual WhatsApp receipts directly to parents.

Measurable Operational Results

Within the first 30 days of live operation, Shishu Vikash Mandir recorded significant operational transformations:

  • ✓
    100% Digital Fee Tracking: Eliminated manual calculation discrepancies. Parents receive instant digital receipts on WhatsApp, establishing complete transparency and reducing fee collection follow-up cycles.
  • ✓
    80% Reduction in Administrative Overhead: Attendance and report card generation—which previously required days of manual collation—now calculate ranks, grades, and marksheet PDFs automatically.
  • ✓
    Zero Data Loss Under Intermittent Internet: Thanks to local cache resilience and atomic Firestore sync, teachers record daily attendance and marks uninterrupted during town power cuts or broadband outages.
“We transitioned our entire school operations to Paathshala smoothly. The offline-first capability is indispensable during local network outages, and parents appreciate receiving verified digital fee receipts on WhatsApp immediately.”
SVM
School Administration Team Shishu Vikash Mandir (Bokaro Steel City)
